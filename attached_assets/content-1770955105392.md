## About Darul Uloom Austin

Darul Uloom Austin (DUA) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit institution with a mission to **prepare future leaders with sound Islamic knowledge and spiritual and ethical values**, rooted in the sacred teachings of the Qur'an and Sunnah.


Over the years, DUA has grown to operate multiple campuses including NAMCC, ICBC, and GIC, offering full-time and part-time Hifdh, After School, and Seminary programs. The institution is powered by dedicated teachers, experienced scholars, and a nurturing community.


#### Our Mission

To prepare future leaders who embody Islamic knowledge and uphold the spiritual and ethical teachings of Islam—empowering them to contribute positively to their families, communities, and the world.

#### Our Growth

Starting in 2007 at NAMCC, DUA has since expanded to include girls and boys full-time programs, Naseeha seminary programs, Deen Intensives, and GIC academic tracks. In 2025, DUA celebrates its 16th graduation with 28 alumni now serving in diverse roles—from Islamic scholarship to industry and higher education.

#### Our Commitment to Quality

DUA is actively improving curriculum and instruction through standardization, teacher training, Tajweed certification, and technology like Muntazim for student tracking. A dedicated Quality Inspector ensures memorization standards and program excellence across campuses.

### Naseeha Program

The Naseeha Program is a two-year foundational part-time course designed to provide students with a deeper understanding of their faith. Whether one aspires to become a scholar or serve the community in other capacities, gaining essential Islamic knowledge is crucial. This program offers a balanced curriculum to nurture both the intellect and character of students on their journey of learning Deen.

**For students 14 years old and above**

**Schedule:**

September to May

Year 1 & 2: Saturdays & Sundays 11:00 AM - 2:00 PM

_Separate Classes for Brothers & Sisters_

**Objectives:**

- To instill a strong foundation in key Islamic sciences
- To strengthen students' understanding of Arabic for deeper connection with the Qur'an and Sunnah
- To develop Islamic character and ethical conduct through Tarbiyat (personal development)
- To prepare students for further Islamic studies or informed engagement in their careers with Islamic values
- To create a space where students can connect knowledge with action and develop a clear religious identity

**Curriculum:**

- Arabic Syntax (ʿIlm al-Naḥw) – Understanding sentence structure to comprehend classical texts
- Morphology (ʿIlm al-Ṣarf) – Learning word patterns and root meanings to enrich vocabulary and interpretation
- Personal Development (Tarbiyat) – Cultivating character, discipline, and spiritual refinement
- Prophetic Narrations (Hadith) – Studying selected ahadith for practical guidance
- Islamic Theology (ʿAqidah) – Learning core beliefs and how to defend them intellectually
- Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh) – Studying everyday rulings and legal principles of Islam
